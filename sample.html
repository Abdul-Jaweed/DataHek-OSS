Principles applied
hierarchy

One accent color (light: yellow · dark: white). Everything else is neutral surface, border and text tiers.

states

Every interactive element has hover, focus-visible, active, disabled and loading states designed — not improvised.

accessibility

AA+ contrast, keyboard everything, aria-live for streaming, role="status" for progress, reduced-motion respected.
